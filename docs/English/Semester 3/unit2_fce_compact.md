### Speaking (Part 2)

#### 1. Gap Fill Exercise

a. In (1) **both** of these photos there are some people eating out, but in this one they're in a street café.

b. In this photo there are two people, but in the (2) **other** one there's a family of three.

c. These two are (3) **similar** in age, about 30, unlike the family inside the restaurant.

d. One (4) **similarity** between the photos is that (5) **both** show people eating a quick lunch.

e. But in some ways the situation in the two photos is quite (6) **different**.

f. The biggest (7) **difference** between them is that this one shows people laughing and enjoying a meal together, but in the (8) **other** one they're looking at their food, not at each other.

#### 2. Comparing Photos 1 and 2

- **a (the place):** Different (outdoor street food truck vs. indoor shop/bakery counter)
- **b (the food):** Different (cooked hot food vs. baked items/groceries)
- **c (the number of people):** Similar (small queue / individual customers)
- **d (the people's age):** Similar (young adults)
- **e (the people's appearance):** Similar (casual daytime clothing)
- **f (what the people are doing):** Same (buying takeaway food/drinks)

#### 3. Photos 3 and 4 (Page 96 Comparison)

- **Similarities:** Both show people dining together in a group, enjoying food, and conversing.
- **Differences:** Photo 3 shows informal outdoor terrace dining, whereas Photo 4 shows an indoor restaurant setting with formal table service.

#### 4–6. Speaking Exam Task Sample Answers

**Candidate A (Photos 1 & 2):**

> "Both photographs show people buying takeaway food, but the settings are quite different. In the first picture, someone is purchasing food directly from an outdoor street truck, whereas in the second picture, a customer is ordering over a counter indoors. People might choose to buy food from a food truck for convenience, speed, and fresh hot meals on the go. On the other hand, buying food inside a shop offers shelter, a wider selection of bakery products, and a more structured environment."

**Candidate B Follow-up Question:** _Do you like to go food shopping?_

> "Personally, I enjoy food shopping when I have enough time to browse fresh markets or local supermarkets, as it gives me ideas for cooking. However, when I am busy, it can feel a bit repetitive."

### Writing (Part 2 - Article)

#### 1. Linking Expressions Matching

1. **Initially** $\rightarrow$ at first
2. **In the meantime** $\rightarrow$ between those two times
3. **Once** $\rightarrow$ as soon as
4. **Instantly** $\rightarrow$ immediately
5. **Simultaneously** $\rightarrow$ at the same time
6. **To my amazement** $\rightarrow$ very surprisingly

#### 2. Task Analysis

1. Young international readers of a website.
2. Mainly about the past.
3. Between 140 and 190 words.

#### 3. Model Article Analysis ("Out at night")

1. **Length:** Yes, around 180 words.
2. **Style:** Informal, engaging, direct.
3. **Hook:** Creates curiosity about who rang the doorbell on a disappointing birthday.
4. **Ending:** Refers back to the initial birthday evening.
5. **Corrections per paragraph:**

- Paragraph 1: _watch TV_ $\rightarrow$ **watching TV**
- Paragraph 2: _how delighting I was_ $\rightarrow$ **how delighted I was**
- Paragraph 3: _dancin_ $\rightarrow$ **dancing**
- Paragraph 4: _for a dinner_ $\rightarrow$ **for dinner**

#### 4. Language Features in Model

1. **Direct address:** _"Have you ever felt..."_, _"You can imagine how delighted I was!"_, _"I think you will agree..."_
2. **Tenses used:**

- Past continuous: _was missing_, _were talking_
- Past perfect: _had given_, _had been talking_, _had been_
- Past perfect continuous: _had been talking_

3. **Linking expressions:**

- _immediately:_ The moment, Instantly
- _after a while:_ before long, after that
- _in the end:_ Eventually

#### 5. Sample Student Article

```text
An Unforgettable Celebration

Have you ever had an unexpected evening out that completely turned your weekend around? That is exactly what happened to me last summer after finishing our end-of-year exams.

Initially, my friends and I had planned a quiet night at home watching movies because we were all exhausted. However, as soon as the last exam ended, my best friend called to announce a surprise change of plan. Instantly, our fatigue vanished as we got ready and headed downtown.

First, we met at a lively food market by the river, where we shared delicious street food while listening to live acoustic music. In the meantime, the atmosphere grew even more dynamic as lights filled the promenade. Later, we walked over to a nearby bowling alley, where we spent two hours laughing, competing, and celebrating the end of term.

Eventually, I arrived home after midnight, completely exhausted but thrilled. I think you will agree that spontaneous outings with close friends make for the best memories!

```
