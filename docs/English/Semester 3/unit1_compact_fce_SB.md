## Grammar (Page 10 & 11)

### 1. Match extracts to rules

- a: (`That north wind brings snow.`) -> 3 (something which is always true / natural law)
- b: (`They're always talking about building more.`) -> 7 (something irritating or surprising, using always)
- c: (`They sell hot food, too.`) -> 2 (a permanent situation / fact)
- d: (`The remaining human inhabitants are starting to follow.`) -> 6 (a situation that is changing or developing)
- e: (`I mainly work from home.`) -> 1 (a routine or habit)
- f: (`I'm calling about my order.`) -> 4 (something happening right now)
- g: (`He's working night shifts all this week.`) -> 5 (a temporary situation)
