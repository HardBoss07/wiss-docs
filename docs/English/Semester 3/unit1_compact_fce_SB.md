## Writing (Page 13)

### Part 2 Informal Email

#### 1. Exam Task Questions

1. Alex (an English friend)
2. Explain importance of friends, best friend, and what you like about them
3. Style features:
   - a: I'm, I've, don't, I'd
   - b: got, kid
   - c: because, so
   - d: -, !
   - e: tell me

#### 2. Model Email Analysis

```text
Dear Alex,

Many thanks for your message. It was great to hear from you!

Friends are extremely important to me. I spend a lot of time with my group during the week, whether we are studying together or just hanging out after class. Life would be boring without them!

My best friend is named Marco, whom I have known since primary school. We live in the same neighborhood and do almost everything together. Even though we get along well, we have very different personalities.

For example, I tend to get quite anxious and impatient when things do not go according to plan, but Marco is always calm and optimistic. He is also incredibly practical and helpful whenever anyone has a problem. Besides that, he has a great sense of humor and always knows how to make me laugh when I feel stressed.

I hope you get to meet him one day! Tell me more about your childhood friends in your next email.

Best wishes,

Matteo Bosshard
```
